Privacy Policy for SkillSprint AI

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

SkillSprint AI ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy while delivering a personalized, intelligent language learning experience. This Privacy Policy details how we handle data within our web and mobile applications.

1. Data Safety & Information We Process

  • Audio & Speech Data: When utilizing the Pronunciation Lab, your microphone audio is processed in real time solely to evaluate phonetic accuracy and fluency. Raw audio waveforms are never permanently archived or distributed to third parties.
  • Account & Profile: Email address and authentication identifiers provided through Google Sign-In or verified phone numbers used for referral security.
  • Learning Progress: SuperMemo SM-2 repetition schedules, vocabulary mastery scores, XP, streak days, and companion preferences.
  • Purchases & Subscriptions: Google Play In-App Billing transaction identifiers. We do not collect or store your payment card numbers.

2. Data Security & Storage

All network communications between SkillSprint AI and our API endpoints are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 protocol. Access to database servers is strictly restricted via role-based access controls (RBAC).

3. Google Play Store Declarations

  • No data is sold to data brokers or advertising networks.
  • Audio recordings are ephemeral and deleted immediately post-analysis.
  • No background location tracking.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may request immediate deletion of your account and all associated study history at any time through the in-app Profile > Settings > Delete Account button or by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@skillsprint.ai.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email support@skillsprint.ai.
